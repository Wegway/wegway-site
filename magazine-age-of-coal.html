Clip Art from the Age of Coal

Clip Art from the Age of Coal was a data CD of etchings and drawings formatted as 300dpi JPGs. They date from the Renaissance to the early 20th century, with the vast majority from 1850 to 1920. All of the images were copyright free, cleared for use however the buyer saw fit, and sorted by category — machines, architecture, animals, transportation, military, plants, and more.

This was a companion project sold alongside the magazine's back issues. It's no longer available for purchase.
