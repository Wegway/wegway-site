NFTs

An NFT as art, now there’s a concept – an address for a decentralized file as art. That sounds like conceptual art to me. And here’s a caveat: I am not a graphic artist, there’s not a lot of skill involved here. But that’s not a serious problem because everything is a bit scruffy if you look close enough.

Deck “a” of 52 individually produced cards is now complete. Deck “b” will perhaps be coming sometime or other, and it’s my ambition to issue unique cards until I mint deck “z” for a grand total of 1,352 (26 x 52) individual cards based on my visual art and written work. This might be a long time coming though. My focus seems to have moved towards quotes and fragments. Time will tell.
